### Identity thesis
This site takes the form of an energized legal field record, because the client can demonstrate concrete legal research, documentation and service experience, and potential employers need to verify those contributions quickly without fabricated case results.

### Seven structural concepts considered inside the user-selected Thunderline art direction
All variants preserve the electric plasma brief, but change the reading grammar and anatomy, not just their palette:

1. **Evidence index**: opens with a dense fact index → skills → supporting records → contacts; typographic grammar data-first tabular references; small-screen risk: density; advantage: rapid recruiter scan.
2. **Legal dossier**: opens with a named cover → experience file folders → organization records → references; grammar archival labels and long captions; risk: artificial legal theatre.
3. **Chronological charge**: opens with the most recent internship → backward timeline → education → contact; grammar dates govern alignment; risk: unbalanced timeline and short experience record.
4. **Field record / selected**: name and role first → verified evidence → actual capability types → chronology → genuine photographs/organizations → external document → contact; grammar high-impact condensed name and concise reading panels; risk: visual intensity if poorly controlled.
5. **Research notebook**: study profile first → methods and competencies → training and placements → personal references; grammar long-form reading; risk: CV does not include enough primary writing samples.
6. **Recruiter comparison table**: side-by-side capability and experience evidence → education → contact; grammar aligned rows; risk: feels like applicant-tracking UI rather than authored personal portfolio.
7. **Editorial photo essay**: photographic identity before copy → people and learning → practice → dates → contact; grammar captions as navigation; risk: visual photography would eclipse legal substance.

**Selected:** Field record. Its evidence-first content sequence supports recruiters and hiring lawyers, while the three supplied images provide a personal visual narrative without inventing new case studies. Alternatives were rejected for the stated weaknesses, not for aesthetic fashion.

### Signature and interaction rationale
**Signature:** A realistic blue/violet plasma field flows behind Rachmat's name, paired with an uninterrupted visible evidence hierarchy. Audience benefit: immediate recognition and memorable identity without hiding legal qualifications. Fallback: static plasma poster plus all HTML content; reduced-motion and pause button. The 3-second video itself is decorative; it conveys no unique fact.

### Anti-generic choices
- No repeated centered hero → three equal services cards → generic CTA skeleton.
- No fictional cases, outcomes, percentages, testimonials, qualifications or quotes.
- A practice-list uses source-backed action descriptions, not generic service packages.
- Timeline distinguishes actual internships from education.
- An additional portfolio link is attributed only as a document listed on the CV; contents not asserted without review.
- Photographs remain authentic source materials, color graded and cropped for composition.

### Visual and technical tradeoffs
Dark electric hero followed by pale evidence-led section, lavender practice editorial, dark timeline and real-life photography. The page alternates density and scale to avoid monochrome repetition. MP4 footage (3 s, no sound) replaces WebGL; 2D video is the only high-impact background media. Keep manual pause, reduced motion, static poster and hidden-tab playback suspension. Quality must be assessed from real rendering, not inferred from this record.
